Small pieces of hardware that clip, click and snap onto the clothes you already own — so every outfit can be upgraded, remixed and made yours.

Clothes are software now. They just don't know it yet.

Your phone gets updates. Your car gets updates. Your jacket? It stays exactly the way it left the factory.

We think that's strange. So we're building hardware for clothing — small, well-made mods that change how your fits look, work and feel. No sewing. No tailor. No throwing things away.

Stugz is for people who treat getting dressed like a creative act. Buy less. Mod more.
